# 🌊 FloodGuard Miami — Predict. Prepare. Protect.

### Overview
FloodGuard Miami is a no-code flood prediction and alert platform designed for Miami, Florida.  
It combines open environmental data, AI-driven risk scoring, and community reporting to help residents and city planners prepare for flooding events in advance.

---

### Problem
Miami faces chronic flooding due to sea-level rise, heavy rainfall, and king tides.  
Existing flood tools are static, outdated, and lack real-time local insights.

---

### Solution
FloodGuard Miami integrates NOAA and USGS data, combines it with local flood reports, and uses no-code AI workflows to generate a neighborhood-level flood risk index.  
The platform visualizes real-time risk on an interactive dashboard and sends alerts to users.

---

### Impact
- Reduce property loss by up to 20% annually  
- Empower residents and city planners with actionable insights  
- Support Miami’s 2050 Climate Resilience goals
