About FloodGuard Miami
The Problem
Miami faces chronic flooding from sea-level rise, heavy rainfall, and king tides. Existing flood tools are static, outdated, and lack real-time local insights that residents and planners actually need.
The Solution
FloodGuard integrates NOAA and USGS data with local flood reports and uses no-code AI workflows to generate a neighborhood-level flood risk index — visualized in real-time and delivered via alerts.
Impact Goals
Reduce property loss by up to 20% annually. Empower residents and city planners with actionable insights. Support Miami's 2050 Climate Resilience goals.
Data Sources
NOAA tidal gauges · USGS stream data · Miami-Dade stormwater network · Community flood reports · National Weather Service rainfall data.
