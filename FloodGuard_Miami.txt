Startup Name: FloodGuard Miami
Predict. Prepare. Protect.

Problem

Miami faces chronic flooding due to sea-level rise, heavy rainfall, and king tides.
Even on sunny days, streets in Brickell, Little River, and Shorecrest experience flooding.
Existing flood maps (like FEMA’s) are outdated, static, and reactive, leaving residents, planners, and businesses without real-time insight.
This lack of localized, predictive data causes property damage, traffic disruptions, and safety risks every year.

Solution

FloodGuard Miami is a no-code flood prediction and alert platform designed for Miami’s communities.
It integrates:

Open data from NOAA, USGS, and local tide sensors

Community flood reports via a web/mobile interface

AI-driven risk scoring to forecast neighborhood-level flooding

Users can view live maps, receive early alerts, and make informed decisions — from homeowners moving cars in time, to city engineers deploying pumps proactively.

Market Opportunity

Miami-Dade County has a $3B climate resilience plan and is among the top 5 most flood-prone U.S. regions.
The climate risk analytics market is projected to reach $12B globally by 2030.
FloodGuard Miami targets:

City governments for predictive dashboards

Real estate & insurance firms for risk assessment

Residents & NGOs for community awareness
